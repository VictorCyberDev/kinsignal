KinSignal — Safety, when you need it.
A unified personal-safety decision and response companion that turns uncertain suspicious situations into transparent warning indicators, actionable steps and explainable records.

## Demonstration scenario (synthetic)
1. On Home choose 'Someone wants my login code'.
2. Assess the situation and note the one-time-code warning.
3. Show unverified sender identity and concrete protective actions.
4. Mark a task user-confirmed complete; the interface does not claim it externally verified.
5. Update the same case to add password exposure and reassess.
6. Explain assessment continuity, preserved history and new action priorities.
7. Prepare a redacted support summary, but do not claim it was delivered.
8. Export the decision receipt.
9. Open Global Safety Atlas and compare country resources and source provenance.
10. Show a fictional lesson and privacy checkup.
